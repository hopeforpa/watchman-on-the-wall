New rules for psychiatric treatment facilities for youth go to a state vote Thursday

The final version sets minimum licensing standards for psychiatric residential treatment facilities. It also sets Medical Assistance participation and payment rules for facilities that treat children, youth and young adults under 21.

Suggestions by section · Hope for PA perspective

What the rule says, and what to ask for

Each point cites the rule's own section so reviewers can check it. Pick the one or two that matter most to you.

§ 5330.31(d) Parents' rights
What it says: Parents have the right to ongoing contact with their child and to actively take part in treatment, unless a court order prohibits it.
Our suggestion: Support this. Ask that a court order stay the only exception, so contact and participation can't be limited by facility policy.
Possible unintended consequence: Without that, a facility's internal policy could narrow these rights over time.
§ 5330.17 Consent to treatment
What it says: The facility must obtain consent before treatment begins.
Our suggestion: Ask the Department to state plainly who gives consent for a minor and how parents are told when treatment or medication changes.
Possible unintended consequence: If it's unclear, families may learn about major changes after the fact.
§ 5330.185(k) Restraint notice
What it says: Parents must be notified within 24 hours when staff use a manual restraint. Other recordable incidents require notice within 12 hours (§ 5330.15(b)).
Our suggestion: Ask that parents be told about any restraint within 12 hours, the same as other incidents.
Possible unintended consequence: A day's delay can mean a parent hears about it from their child first.
§ 5330.18 Confidentiality
What it says: Facilities must follow existing confidentiality laws. The final version relies on those laws rather than listing who may receive a child's records.
Our suggestion: Ask that the rule name who may receive records and require that parents be told when records are shared outside the facility.
Possible unintended consequence: Privacy protection then depends on other laws that can change without review of this rule.
§ 5330.31(b)(14) Religion
What it says: A child may practice their religion of choice or abstain from religious practice.
Our suggestion: Support this. Ask the Department to confirm that faith-based providers may offer voluntary spiritual care that respects this right and parents' wishes.
Possible unintended consequence: Unclear guidance could discourage faith-based programs from taking part.
§ 5330.42 Staffing, accreditation and cost
What it says: 1 staff per 6 youth while awake, 1 per 12 while asleep, plus required accreditation. The Department estimates about $24.5 million a year in added cost and gave existing facilities 24 months to comply.
Our suggestion: Ask the Department to track bed capacity publicly during the 24 months, and to keep waivers (§ 5330.231) open to small, rural and faith-based providers.
Possible unintended consequence: If programs close or shrink, children may wait longer for care or be placed farther from home.

Section references are from the final regulation text (PDF).

Suggested comment · Hope for PA perspective

Say it in your own words

Built from the sections above. Use it as a starting point, then add a sentence about your own family or community. Comments in your own words count more than copies.

RE: REGULATION #14-555 (IRRC #3417)
To the Independent Regulatory Review Commission: I am a Pennsylvania [parent / grandparent / resident] from [your town], writing about Regulation #14-555, Psychiatric Residential Treatment Facilities. I'm grateful this rule recognizes that children heal best with their families. Parents' right to ongoing contact and active participation in treatment (§ 5330.31(d)) honors the God-given role of parents, and I ask that a court order remain the only exception. I respectfully ask for three changes: 1. Notify parents of any manual restraint within 12 hours, the same as other incidents (§ 5330.185(k), § 5330.15(b)). 2. State plainly who consents to a minor's treatment, and how parents are told when treatment or medication changes (§ 5330.17). 3. Name who may receive a child's records, and tell parents when records are shared outside the facility (§ 5330.18). I also ask the Commission to weigh the cost. The Department estimates about $24.5 million a year in new costs. Please make sure the staffing and accreditation rules (§ 5330.42) don't close small, rural or faith-based programs and leave children waiting longer or farther from home. [One or two sentences from your own experience.] Thank you for your careful review. [Your name] [Your town], Pennsylvania
Tips that work
  • Always include the regulation number
  • Add one true sentence about your family or town
  • Stay kind and specific. Name the part you support or question.
  • Share only what you're comfortable making public. Comments go on IRRC's website.
